Narcotics — RimWorld 1.6
by Anarchist

Adds real-world-inspired narcotics using vanilla drug systems: highs, tolerance, addiction, withdrawal, and overdose — plus doctor prescriptions, a meth lab, growable psychs, and drug-dealer caravans.

[h1]What's included[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Percocet / Xanax[/b] — Medical pills at the drug lab
[*][b]Weed / Cocaine[/b] — Growables (cocaine refined at drug lab)
[*][b]Fentanyl[/b] — Strong opioid; can anesthetize
[*][b]Meth[/b] — Cooked only at a [b]meth lab[/b]. Extremely addictive hard stimulant
[*][b]Psychedelic mushrooms[/b] — Grow and eat for a vivid trip
[*][b]Salvia[/b] — Short trip; users may believe they are a random object
[/list]

[h1]Drug dealers[/h1]
Occasional dealer caravans stock [b]every[/b] Narcotics drug. Addicts with no colony supply may automatically buy from the dealer.

[h1]Prescriptions[/h1]
Doctors can prescribe any Narcotics drug for 3 or 7 days under Operations.

[h1]Research[/h1]
Drug production (Main tab), then the Narcotics tab. Meth unlocks the meth lab. Growables need no research.
